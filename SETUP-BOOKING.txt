HOW TO CHANGE THINGS (no coding needed)

1. WORDS  -> open  site-text.js
   Every word on every page is in that one file (home page, the 5 booking pages,
   cancellation policy, owner's bookings page). Change only the text between
   the quote marks. The top of the file explains the 7 simple rules.

2. PRICES -> open  pricing.json
   Only numbers live there (adult/child prices, extras, thali and bamboo dish prices,
   minimum advance). The names inside it are only for your booking alert.

3. PAYMENT DETAILS (UPI ID, bank rows, QR file name) -> site-text.js, section "payment".
   Put your QR picture at images/payment-qr.png

4. Adding a new extra or dish: add it in pricing.json (with its price)
   AND give it a name in site-text.js under the same short name.

5. Videos for the 3 package cards: book.html, the FLEET list near the bottom.
